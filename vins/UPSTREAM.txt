VINS-Mono, for phones that have no ARCore
========================================

What is here
------------
`src/main/cpp/upstream/` is VINS-Mono, the monocular visual-inertial estimator of the HKUST Aerial
Robotics Group, copied from

    https://github.com/HKUST-Aerial-Robotics/VINS-Mono        commit 90dabb5ec79946ae42fd2e1e91d4e69aabe1e25d

and left as it is upstream except where an Android build forces a change. `src/main/cpp/upstream/
COPYING` is its licence text; read the note at the end of this file before shipping a build with it.

Which parts are needed, and which were left out
-----------------------------------------------
VINS-Mono upstream is a set of ROS packages. The AR demo of the repository (`ar_demo/`) is one of
them: it subscribes to the estimator's `odometry` topic, projects a virtual cube with the camera model
and draws it over the images. There is no Android Studio project in the official repository, so what
this module does is what the demo does with the pose — take the estimator's output as the headset's
6DoF — and it feeds the estimator the way `ar_demo`'s launch files do, from a config YAML.

Built here:
  camera_model/      the pinhole and fisheye camera models, and the YAML reader for them
                     (without its chessboard calibration tool: a phone is calibrated by the platform)
  feature_tracker/   the KLT front end
                     (without its ROS node: `runtime/vins_frontend.cpp` is that node, in C++)
  vins_estimator/    IMU pre-integration, automatic initialisation, the sliding-window optimiser,
                     marginalisation, failure detection and recovery
                     (without its ROS node, its rviz publishers and its parameter reader, which are
                     `runtime/vins_backend.cpp` and `runtime/vins_config.cpp`; the pose graph and
                     loop closure are `pose_graph/`, and are not built — see the limits below)

Not taken at all: `benchmark_publisher/`, `data_generator/`, `docker/`, `support_files/`, the bags.

Touched, and why
----------------
  vins_estimator/src/parameters.h    `ROW`/`COL` are ints, to match the feature tracker's own
                                     declaration of the same two variables. Upstream keeps them in two
                                     nodes that never meet; on a phone they share one process, and the
                                     linker only tolerates one definition of each.
  everything else                    untouched. ROS is not patched out of the sources but shimmed
                                     next to them, in `src/main/cpp/shim/`: `ros/ros.h`,
                                     `ros/console.h`, `ros/assert.h`, `std_msgs/Header.h`,
                                     `std_msgs/Float32.h`, `sensor_msgs/Imu.h`,
                                     `sensor_msgs/PointCloud.h`, `geometry_msgs/*` are the few
                                     declarations the estimator actually reads, and `boost/*` is what
                                     camodocal's headers ask for (`std::shared_ptr` and one
                                     case-insensitive compare). So the vendored files still compile
                                     against upstream's own headers, and a future resync is a copy.

Written here (all of it NextVR's, not upstream's): `src/main/cpp/runtime/` and `src/main/cpp/jni/`.

Licence
-------
VINS-Mono is GPL-3.0 (the older copies of the file say GPL-2.0; this tree says GPL-3.0). Linking it
into an APK makes that APK a work with the same obligations: the source of the whole app has to be
offered under the GPL when the APK is given out. NextVR's repository is public, and the module can be
left out of a build with `-Pvins.enabled=false` (see `vins/build.gradle.kts`), which is the switch to
reach for when a build must not carry the licence — the app then keeps ARCore and its own 3DoF
tracking, and the settings screen says why the mode is unavailable.

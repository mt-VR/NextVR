PXRCraft is QuestCraft ported to phones with PhoneXR.

QuestCraft: https://github.com/QuestCraftPlusPlus
The launcher (Unity): https://github.com/QuestCraftPlusPlus/QCXR-XR-Wrapper — LGPL-3.0
Pojlib (running Minecraft): https://github.com/QuestCraftPlusPlus/Pojlib — LGPL-3.0
Vivecraft, LWJGL, OpenJDK and the other components are under their own licenses, see the QuestCraft repositories.

In the official release, build_pxrcraft.py changes only the package name, the title, the icon, the manifest
requirements and the OpenXR loader. The sources of the components are available at the links above.

Minecraft belongs to Mojang/Microsoft. Playing requires your own purchased copy of Minecraft: Java Edition.
